How this works. Data comes from OpenRouter's public rankings. Spend shares in the labs and models tables are OpenRouter's own numbers for yesterday, the last 7 days and the last 30 days. Daily dollar amounts are estimates: each model's daily input, cached-input and output tokens priced at OpenRouter list prices, then scaled so each model's 30-day total matches its reported spend share. OpenRouter publishes 31 days of daily detail, so this site saves a snapshot every 6 hours and keeps history from then on. The 1-year view is tokens only and groups everyone outside each week's top 9 makers into Other.
