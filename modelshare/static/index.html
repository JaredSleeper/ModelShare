How this works. Data comes from OpenRouter's public rankings. Spend shares in the labs and models tables are OpenRouter's own numbers for yesterday, the last 7 days and the last 30 days. Daily dollar amounts are estimates: each model's daily input, cached-input and output tokens priced at OpenRouter list prices, then scaled so each model's 30-day total matches its reported spend share. OpenRouter publishes 31 days of daily detail, so this site saves a snapshot every 6 hours and keeps history from then on. The 1-year view is tokens only and groups everyone outside each week's top 9 makers into Other. The since-2023 view uses actual weekly spend per model that OpenRouter's rankings page carried until August 2025, read from Internet Archive copies of the page (weekly totals through mid-January 2025, then 7-day totals from about one copy a week), followed by this site's own data once a full week is saved. The page stopped including dollars in late August 2025, so the months in between are blank. The monthly spend download fills that gap with estimates: each lab's weekly tokens from OpenRouter's chart times its $/token, where $/token comes from list prices applied to the page's full 7-day token table (Aug 2025 to May 2026), scaled by how list prices compared with reported spend in mid-2025, and blended between the nearest known weeks after that. Its basis column says which days are reported, estimated or this site's own data. Labs outside a week's top 9 makers are in Other.
